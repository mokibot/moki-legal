Moki — Privacy Policy Last Updated: [16 September 2026] This Privacy Policy explains what information Moki may process, why it is processed, how it is used, and the choices available to users. By using Moki, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. Information Moki Processes When you interact with Moki, information provided through Discord may be processed. This may include: Discord user ID; Discord username; display name; avatar; server ID; server name; channel IDs; message IDs where required; Discord permission information where required for authorization. Moki may also store information created through use of the Bot. This may include: Moki Coin balance; bank balance; transaction history; game history; wagers; game results; lottery tickets; lottery winnings; Loot Keys; Loot Crates; Luck; streak information; current and best streak; level; qualifying message count; achievements; titles; missions; cooldowns; heist activity; profile statistics; moderation status; Moki account creation/activity information. 2. Message Processing Moki may process Discord messages when necessary to operate features. For example, Moki may process messages to: detect prefix commands such as moki help; respond to commands; count qualifying messages toward Moki levels; detect money drop claims; prevent spam or abuse; enforce cooldowns; operate game or progression features. Moki is not intended to permanently archive ordinary Discord conversations. Where possible, progression systems use identifiers, counters, timestamps, and other metadata rather than storing the full content of normal conversations. 3. Economy Information Moki stores virtual economy information necessary to operate the service. This may include: current balances; transfers; purchases; game payouts; heist payouts; lottery activity; crate rewards; administrator adjustments; other economy transactions. This information may be retained to prevent duplication, investigate abuse, maintain transaction integrity, and troubleshoot problems. 4. Level and Progression Data Moki may track qualifying Discord activity for its global leveling system. This may include: qualifying message count; level; progression toward the next level; level reward status; global ranking. Anti-spam systems may reject repeated, automated, or unusually rapid activity from counting toward progression. 5. Server Configuration When server administrators configure Moki, the Bot may store: server ID; selected channel IDs; enabled features; setup settings; announcement preferences; feature configuration. These settings allow Moki to continue operating correctly after restarts. 6. Security and Abuse Information To protect users and Moki’s virtual economy, Moki may process information relating to suspicious activity. This may include: unusual economy activity; unusually large rewards; duplicate interaction attempts; repeated invalid commands; exploit attempts; cooldown bypass attempts; suspicious transfers; administrator actions; bans or restrictions; server economy anomalies. Relevant user IDs, server IDs, timestamps, transaction values, and activity details may be retained for security and investigation purposes. 7. Economy Alerts Moki may generate automated security or economy alerts for authorized Moki administrators. For example, alerts may be generated where: a user earns an unusually large amount of virtual currency; a server experiences unusually high virtual currency creation; a large administrative adjustment occurs; Moki detects abnormal economic activity. These alerts are intended to protect Moki’s economy and investigate possible technical problems or abuse. 8. Why Information Is Processed Moki processes information in order to: provide Discord bot functionality; maintain virtual balances; operate games; operate streaks and Loot Crates; calculate levels; provide leaderboards; operate missions and achievements; maintain server configuration; prevent duplicate rewards; prevent abuse and exploitation; enforce restrictions; maintain transaction integrity; investigate bugs; secure the service; improve Moki. Where applicable, processing may rely on providing the requested service and legitimate interests in maintaining and securing Moki. 9. Publicly Displayed Information Certain Moki features intentionally show information to other Discord users. This may include: display name; username; avatar; level; leaderboard position; Moki Coin balance; streak; achievements; titles; game results; lottery winnings; records; profile statistics. Moki's leaderboard system is designed to display users without unnecessarily pinging them. 10. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed through services necessary to provide Moki, including: Discord; Moki’s hosting provider; infrastructure providers used to run Moki. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, security threats, or unlawful activity. 11. Data Retention Moki account and economy data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service. Some information may remain longer where required for: transaction integrity; fraud or abuse prevention; moderation history; security; backups; troubleshooting; legal obligations. Backups may temporarily contain previous versions of information after live data has been changed or deleted. Information that is no longer reasonably necessary should be removed or anonymized where appropriate. 12. Data Security Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Moki data. These may include: database transactions; restricted administrator access; permission checks; database backups; integrity checks; audit logs; duplicate transaction protection; abuse detection; secure random generation; administrator authentication. However, no online system can guarantee complete security. 13. Data Requests Depending on applicable law, users may have rights relating to their personal information. These may include rights to request: access; correction; deletion; restriction; objection; information about how data is used. Requests can be submitted through the Moki support server or contact email. Identity verification may be required before a request is completed. 14. Data Deletion Users may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with their Moki account. Deleting Moki data may permanently remove or reset: coins; bank balance; levels; message progression; streaks; keys; crates; achievements; titles; missions; statistics; other progression. Certain records may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction integrity, legal obligations, or protection of the service. 15. Removing Moki From a Server Removing Moki from a Discord server prevents Moki from continuing to operate in that server. Some configuration or historical information may remain temporarily for: backups; security; troubleshooting; data integrity. Server owners may contact Moki support regarding server configuration information. 16. Children Moki is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under Discord’s applicable age requirements. Moki is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from anyone who is not legally permitted to use Discord. If you believe information relating to such a person has been collected, please contact us. 17. International Data Processing Discord, hosting providers, and infrastructure services may operate in multiple countries. As a result, information may be processed outside the user’s country of residence subject to applicable safeguards. 18. Third-Party Services Moki relies on Discord and may use third-party hosting or infrastructure providers. Their processing of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. 19. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as Moki develops or its data practices change. Significant changes may be announced through: Moki; the Moki support server; the Moki website; another appropriate method. 20.
